  1,2» on the first line and «Мп» alone on the second (`g3_chip_ru_crop.webp`). No overlap with «Выбрать другое фото», so G3
  passes. EN wraps at the bullet («960 × 1280 ·» / «1.2 MP»), which keeps the value whole. Expected: a non-breaking space
  between «1,2» and «Мп».
- Observation: the estimate was 1.09× optimistic on this run: «≈ 4 minutes 7 seconds» (247 s) against a real **4 min 29 s**
  (269 s). RC2 on this phone gave 1.02–1.04 (estimates ≈ 4:10–4:15 against 4:17–4:24). The RC3 bench came out a little faster
  (gpu_ms 2657 vs 2684, ESRGAN correction 1.30 vs 1.36), so the estimate shrank while the run grew longer. This run came right
  after the cancelled run, with the battery at 31 °C.
- Observation: the first-launch bench takes 14.8 s (RC2 15.9 s).
- Observation: in EN the expanded-card chip takes two lines («960 × 1280 ·» / «1.2 MP»). It is readable, with no overlap.
- Observation (test tooling, not the app): `ui "wait Close 3000"` answered «found» with no dialog on screen, and `labels`
  right after an (i) tap sometimes threw `StaleObjectException`. Each of the 14 texts was confirmed by a `labels` read that
