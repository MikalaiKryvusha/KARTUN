## 5. Found

No bug documents were filed: this run was report-only. Each item below is for the owner to triage.

1. **(minor) The model-card time estimate breaks between a number and its unit.** Enhance, model cards, «For compressed, blurry
   photos from the web · ≈ 4» / «minutes 9 seconds».
   - EN portrait: the Nomos card (`h3_en_nomos_crop.webp`).
   - RU portrait: «…из интернета · ≈ 4» / «минуты 9 секунд» (`h3_ru_nomos_crop.webp`).
   - EN landscape: Nomos «≈ 4 minutes 9» / «seconds», Siax «≈ 9» / «minutes 8 seconds» (`m_h4_en_enh.webp`).
   - RU landscape: «≈ 4 минуты 9» / «секунд», «≈ 9» / «минут 8 секунд» (`m_h4_ru_enh.webp`).

   It already existed in RC3: `rc3/redmi/g2_enhance_en.webp` shows «≈ 4» / «minutes 7 seconds». The RC3 report did not list it.
   Expected: non-breaking spaces inside «≈ 4 minutes» and «9 seconds».
2. **(minor) Hindi: the Siax estimate breaks between «8» and «सेकंड».** Enhance, portrait, HI: «… के लिए · ≈ 9 मिनट 8» / «सेकंड»
   (`h3_hi_siax_crop.webp`). It is the same cause as item 1. Hindi was not checked in RC3.
3. **(medium) Landscape result screen: the photo is nearly invisible.** EN, landscape, after the run. The info panel takes about
   2/3 of the height, and the before/after photo is squeezed into a ≈ 275 × 365 px strip at the top centre. The pink divider knob
   covers most of that strip. «Before» and «After» sit at the far left and right screen edges, ≈ 900 px away from the photo
   (`h4_en_result.webp`, `h4_en_result_top_crop.webp`, tree `h4_result_tree.txt`). The user cannot compare the result in landscape.
   Not checked in RC3 (no landscape there).
4. **(minor) The live «ELAPSED» counter falls behind after a configuration change.** Processing, EN, portrait, after rotating to
   landscape and back. Before the rotation «3 s» at 3.5 s real. After it «12 s» at ≈ 17 s real, and the gap stayed at 5–9 s to the
   end («4 min 25 s» at 274 s, `h8_poll.txt`). After the AR switch, ELAPSED read «١ د ٢٨ ث» and then «1 min 28 s» 3 s later.
   The final «4 minutes 34 seconds» on the result is correct (273.8 s by logcat). Not checked in RC3.
