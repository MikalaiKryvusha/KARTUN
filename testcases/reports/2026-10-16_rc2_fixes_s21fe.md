## 5. Found

No bug docs were filed: this run's brief was to report, not to edit docs. Each item gives the place in the code, for the bug doc.

1. **Enhance collapsed photo row clips the result size, and the name shows only «IMG_20…»** (layout, F3, all languages at 360 dp).
   The row has a fixed height (`SetupScreen.kt`: `Modifier.height(heroDp)` with `HERO_MIN = 80.dp` and `padding(8.dp)`). The
   `SizeChange` column that falls to three lines (`Common.kt` `SizeChange`) plus the name line is taller than about 64 dp, so
   the third line «3840 × 5120» is cut through its glyphs. The `maxLines = 1` name gets only the space left of «Another photo».
   Evidence: `f3_en_photorow_crop.webp`, `f3_de_enhance.webp`, `f6_ru_enhance.webp`, `f8_ar_photorow_crop.webp`.
2. **Arabic: the photo row's three-line sizes are reversed, «1280 × 960» / «5120 × 3840»** (RTL, F8). The three-line branch of
   `SizeChange` renders `boldDigits(from)` / `boldDigits(to)` without `ltr(…)`, while the one-line branch isolates them. The
   accessibility labels confirm it: `960 × 1280` there has no ⁦⁩, whereas the scale row's has. Evidence: `f8_ar_photorow_crop.webp`.
3. **Arabic About: the version reads «(268) 0.1»** (RTL, minor, F8). `AboutScreen.kt:97`
   `stringResource(R.string.about_tagline, version)` passes `version` without `ltr(…)`, although the RC2 commit says versions were isolated.
   Evidence: `f8_ar_about.webp`.
4. **Arabic scale slider: the pink fill is mirrored but the labels and dots are not** (RTL, minor, new). At 4× the pink fill
   covers 4×→16× on the right, while the labels run «×1 … ×16» left to right and the active dots are 1× and 2× on the left.
   Evidence: `f8_ar_scale_crop.webp`.
5. **The result's divider line shows faintly through the bottom info panel** (cosmetic, all languages). The pink line runs on
   under the panel down to the buttons. Evidence: `f4_result_en.webp`, `f3_de_result_panel.webp`.
6. **Landscape home at rest still cuts the chip row in half** (cosmetic, F10 passes on the case's wording). The labels are readable
   and the column now scrolls, but nothing hints that there is more below the half-drawn pills. Evidence: `f10_land_home.webp`.
