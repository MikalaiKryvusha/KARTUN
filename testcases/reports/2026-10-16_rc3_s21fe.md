## 5. Found

No bug docs were filed: this run's brief was to report, not to edit docs. Each item gives the place in the code, for the bug doc.

1. **Expanded photo card: on 360 dp the size chip wraps inside «960 × 1280» and splits «1,2 / Мп»** (layout, G3; en and ru
   worst). The RC3 row is `SetupScreen.kt:294-300`: `Pill(… Modifier.weight(1f, fill = false))`, then a `Spacer(weight 0.001f)`,
   then the «Choose another photo» / «Выбрать другое фото» button at its full intrinsic width. The pill gets what is left, about
   100 dp, and its text breaks at ordinary spaces. Overlap is gone. A fix needs non-breaking spaces inside the size and inside
   «1,2 Мп», or a size-only chip and a shorter button on narrow screens. Evidence: `g3_ru_chip_crop.webp`, `g3_chips_en_de_ar.webp`.
2. **The ESRGAN family probe is one noisy sample, and here it made the Siax estimate 30 % short** (estimate, new). On the same
   phone, the RC2 first launch measured `base_ms 262 / esrgan_ms 410`, ratio 1.56 and correction 0.85. This RC3 first launch
   measured `base_ms 474 / esrgan_ms 494`, ratio 1.04 and correction 0.563. The base pass nearly doubled, and the clamp
   `MIN_CORRECTION 0.5` barely held. The result: Siax was shown as faster than NomosWebPhoto (1:42 vs 1:52), but it took
   2:26 against 1:25. At RC2 its catalogue estimate was 2:20. Cause: `DeviceBench.kt:85-90` divides two single passes, and
   the base pass is the default model's second side in the same load (`time(… sides = listOf(GPU_SIDE, PROBE_SIDE))`). The
   learned rate corrects this after the first real run, but the first estimate the user sees is wrong. Evidence: `g1_logcat.txt`;
   RC2 `f1_logcat.txt`.
3. **Collapsed row: the file name wraps at any character, splitting the extension «.j / pg»** (cosmetic, G2 en). The name is
   whole as the case requires, but the break falls inside «.jpg» in en («IMG_202610 / 16_220332.j / pg») and inside the date
   in de and ar. Evidence: `g2_en_photorow_crop.webp`.
4. **Russian models list: «· 15 / МБ»** (cosmetic). The V2 Lite description breaks between the number and its unit.
   Evidence: `g6_models_ru.webp`.
5. **Share takes about 14 s of «Rendering…» before the sheet** (observation, not a G9 failure). Share was tapped at 22:11:55
   and the chooser started at 22:12:09.6 with `image/png`. The RC2 report saw about 5 s on this phone. The label behaviour
   the case asks for is correct.

The RC2 findings on this device, rechecked: Found 1 (row clips the three-line size, name «IMG_20…») **fixed**; Found 2
(Arabic sizes reversed in the row) **fixed**; Found 3 (Arabic About «(268) 0.1») **fixed**; Found 4 (Arabic slider fill
mirrored) **fixed**; Found 5 (divider through the panel) **fixed**; Found 6 (landscape home) was not in the RC3 cases and was
not rechecked.
