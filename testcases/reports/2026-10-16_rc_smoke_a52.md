## 5. Found

- **#1 Result screen: the size line collides with the time text at 1080 px** (cosmetic). Steps: C4 on a 1080 × 2400
  phone → result. Expected: «960 × 1280 → 3840 × 5120» and «6 minutes 24 seconds · GPU · Vulkan» readable, apart.
  Actual: the size wraps into three lines («960 × 1280» / «→ 3840 ×» / «5120») and «1280» touches «6 minutes…» with no gap
  (`c4_result_sizeline.webp`). Same family as the Redmi's wrap note; the uncommitted `ResultScreen.kt` change in the tree
  may already address it — not verified on this build.
- **#2 Arabic About: «14 (API 34)» breaks into «API) 14» / «(34»** (cosmetic, RTL bidi). Steps: `set-app-locales … ar` →
  Settings → About. Expected: «14 (API 34)» on one line in LTR order. Actual: parentheses reordered and the value wrapped
  though the row has room (`c10_ar_about_android_row.webp`).
- **#3 Cancel during processing gives no feedback until the tile ends** (low). Steps: Enhance on the A52, at ≥ 10 % press
  «Cancel». Expected: the screen reacts at once (or shows «Cancelling…»). Actual: the progress screen with a live «Cancel»
  stays ≈ 4.6 s (tile 5/35 finishing; tiles take 5–10 s on this GPU) before returning to Enhance.
- **#4 Bench dialog: the spinner's squares are drawn over the title text** (cosmetic, maybe by design). Steps: C2 →
  screenshot at +2 s. Expected: the animation stays clear of the text. Actual: a square covers letters of «performance…»
  and «device's» (`c2_bench_dialog_crop.webp`).
- **#5 The time estimate is still optimistic on this weak device** (soft). Bench-based estimate 5 min 9 s vs real 6 min
  24 s (×1.24); after one run it learns ≈ 5 min 47 s vs real 6 min 24 s (×1.11). Far better than the Redmi's ×3.3 before
  the bench; the coordinator's earlier run on the A52 shows the same ×1.27 (313 vs 397 s).
