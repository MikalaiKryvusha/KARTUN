## 5. Found

No bug docs were filed: this run's brief was to report, not to edit docs.

1. **Expanded photo card (portrait, 360 dp): the size chip still breaks inside «1280»** (R2; en and ru; RC5 Found 1 not fixed).
   It reads «960 × 128 / 0 · 1.2 MP» and «960 × 128 / 0 · 1,2 Мп». The button now wraps to 2 lines as intended, but the chip
   stays as narrow as before. Evidence: `r2_chip_en_ru_crop.webp`, `r2_expanded_en.webp`, `r2_expanded_ru.webp`.
   Hypothesis from reading the diff only (not verified): in `SetupScreen.kt` `PhotoCard`, the button's cap is
   `rowW - sizeLineW - 8.dp`. The Row has three children (Pill, `Spacer(weight 0.001f)`, button) with `spacedBy(8.dp)`, so there
   are **two** 8 dp gaps, and the Pill gets about 8 dp less than `sizeLineW`. Also, `sizeLineW` is measured with plain
   `labelMedium` + `boldDigits`. If `Pill(heavyDigits = true)` draws with a wider style, the measured width is short as well.
2. **Observation (accessibility):** on the result screen, the panel's hide arrow is announced as «Back», the same as the
   screen's back button (`tree`: `Back@2190 523` in landscape, `Back@930 1783` in portrait). Its counterpart is labelled
   «Show the summary». Evidence: `r6_land_tree.txt`. In RC5: not checked.

The RC5 findings on this device, rechecked: Found 1 (chip inside «1280») **not fixed** (Found 1 above). Found 2 (estimate
splits a number from its unit) **fixed** in hi, ar, bn (portrait) and en, ru (landscape). Found 3 (landscape result photo a
small strip) **fixed**. Found 4 (ru licence chip «WTF / PL») **fixed**.
