## 5. Found

1. **Arabic: Before/After labels are swapped against the picture on the result screen** (functional, RTL).
   - Description: in RTL the two labels mirror, but the image halves do not. Each label ends up over the wrong half.
   - Steps: get a result, then `cmd locale set-app-locales com.kriniks.kartun --locales ar`, then look at the result screen.
   - Expected: «قبل» (Before) over the original half and «بعد» (After) over the enhanced half (or the image mirrored with the labels).
   - Actual: «بعد» (After) is on the left and «قبل» (Before) on the right. The pixels of the image area are identical to the RU screen, hash `b5ee2440…` on the same patch for ru and ar, where «До» (Before) is on the left. So the Arabic user is told the original is the result. Evidence: `c14_ar_result.webp`, `top_compare.webp`.
2. **Landscape home: the share-hint chip row is clipped** (layout).
   - Description: the chips at the bottom of the «Or send a photo from another app» card are cut off in landscape.
   - Steps: `settings put system accelerometer_rotation 0; settings put system user_rotation 1`, then open KARTUN.
   - Expected: the chips «Photo → Share → KARTUN» are fully visible.
   - Actual: only the top edge of the three pills shows; the labels are gone (absent from the accessibility tree too). Evidence: `c11_land_home.webp`, `c11_land_home_crop.webp`.
3. **Share sheet shows no file name and no preview on Samsung One UI (Android 16)** (soft).
   - Description: the system share sheet does not show which file is being shared.
   - Steps: on the result screen tap «Share».
   - Expected: the sheet names the PNG (as on the Redmi) and shows its thumbnail.
   - Actual: the heading is «1 image», the preview area is empty with a copy icon, and there is no file name. Unverified hypothesis from reading `ResultScreen.kt:132`: the `ACTION_SEND` intent sets only `EXTRA_STREAM`, with no `ClipData` and no title extra, so the chooser may not be able to read the URI for a preview. Evidence: `c6_share.webp`, `c6_share2.webp`.
4. **French home title overflows the card** (layout).
   - Steps: `--locales fr`, open the home screen.
   - Expected: «Choisissez une photo» centred inside the card, like the other languages.
   - Actual: it wraps to two left-aligned lines starting on the card's left border, with no inner padding. Evidence: `c14_fr_home.webp`, `c14_fr_home_crop.webp`.
5. **German Settings: «GPU-Beschleunigung» broken mid-word** as «GPU-Beschleuni / gung», with no hyphen at the break (`c14_de_settings.webp`).
6. **Size line wraps inside a dimension** on Enhance and Result («3840 ×⏎5120») in en, es, pt, fr, de, zh, hi, ar. The Redmi report noted the same at phone width, and it is worse here at 360 dp (`c4_result.webp`, `m_result1.webp`).
7. **Enhance CTA label wraps to two lines** in en, ru, es, pt, fr, de when the estimate is «≈ 1 minute 25 seconds». The sparkle icon sits against the pill's left edge (`c14_btn_compare.webp`).
8. **Processing screen truncates at 360 dp**: «The network is enhancing yo…», «GPU ·⏎Vulkan» wrapped, and «LEFT ≈ 1 mi…» cut (`c4_processing.webp`).

Observations, not defects: the RC is versionCode 1 / «0.1 (1)» (the commit-count fix came after this APK). The GPU bench on
this build measures a 512 px output, while CPU measures 128 px (`blobstat` lines); the later commit `eb7b246` says «GPU 128 px».
After a bench «Cancel», the GPU pass runs on for ≈ 1.5 s. In landscape the Enhance right pane drops «SCALE» and «All models»
below the fold (scrollable).
